## How hard was it

Harder than the first dogfood. The code-level bugs are largely gone: the
13-state invariant sweep, the 210-pick replay, and the cold-cache launch all
came back clean, and the two defects found *during* the replay were fixed on
the spot. What remains splits into: one genuine modelling defect (H1) that is
visible on any real board the moment humans deviate from ADP; one algorithmic
weakness (H2) that only shows on dense positions; draft-day usability gaps
(H3–H5) rather than errors; latent reliability (H6, H7); and correctness
limits for league shapes this app has not been used with (H8, H9). No panics,
no data-integrity faults, no crashes were found. For a two-day-old codebase
that is a solid state — the model layer is where the real work is, and the UI
is down to papercuts.
